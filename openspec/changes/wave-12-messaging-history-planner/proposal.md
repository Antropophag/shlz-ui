## Why

Messaging / History / Planner compositions are the final `INVENTORIED` family in the component-audit roadmap. Wave 12 must establish three independent source and repository-absence dispositions without turning static application mockups into unsupported design-system behavior or allowing one sub-scope's evidence to certify another.

## What Changes

- Inspect `Messages.svg`, `History of changes.svg`, and `Planner.svg` as the separate visual authorities for Messaging, History, and Planner composition boundaries.
- Census and classify every repository-local composition root, implementation, consumer, fixture, diagnostic, native substitute, and local alternative for each sub-scope.
- Add one family manifest with independent Messaging, History, and Planner source/absence, occurrence, evidence, limitation, and finding ledgers; reconcile the project inventory and publish the Wave 12 report.
- Add mutation-sensitive source-integrity and repository-census evidence without adding runtime product behavior or a public composition API.
- Keep `shlz-design-source/` unchanged and retain the independent status of all nested verified primitives.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `application-compositions/messaging-history-planner-compositions`: Separate source-defined boundaries, repository occurrence classification, and completion evidence for Messaging, History, and Planner compositions.

None.

## Impact

The change affects OpenSpec planning, a new component-audit manifest, the project inventory, focused source/census tests, and the Wave 12 audit report. It adds no editor commands, message delivery/read/synchronization behavior, chronology or scheduling engine, employee data model, attachment lifecycle, persistence, live-region contract, package export, or generic domain API; authoritative SVGs remain read-only.
