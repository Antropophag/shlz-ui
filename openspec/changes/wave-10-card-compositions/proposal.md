## Why

Card compositions are the first source-only family in the remaining audit roadmap and are still merely `INVENTORIED`. Wave 10 must establish a source-traceable absence disposition without turning three static Figma exports into an unsupported reusable Card API or interaction model.

## What Changes

- Inspect `Card with button.svg`, `Reports card.svg`, and `Cover.svg` as the sole visual authorities and record content, image, action, report-card, and cover facts separately from derived patterns and unknown semantics.
- Census and classify every repository-local production implementation, consumer, fixture, diagnostic, native substitute, and local card/cover alternative, including unrelated uses of “card” that must not be mistaken for this family.
- Add an independent Card compositions audit manifest, source/absence evidence, inventory reconciliation, and Wave 10 report with reasoned `not-applicable` evidence levels.
- Verify source integrity and make the absence census mutation-sensitive without adding runtime markup, styles, behavior, package exports, or a generic public Card contract.
- Keep verified nested primitives as regression dependencies and leave `shlz-design-source/` unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `application-compositions/card-compositions`: Source-defined Card, Reports card, and Cover composition boundaries, repository-wide absence classification, and the evidence required for a verified source-only disposition.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

The change affects OpenSpec planning, a new audit manifest, the project inventory, source/census tests, and the Wave 10 audit report. It adds no framework-neutral package export, runtime consumer, interaction behavior, data model, navigation contract, or responsive API; authoritative SVGs remain read-only.
